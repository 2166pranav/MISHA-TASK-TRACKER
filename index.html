FAQs

Have questions? Find quick answers before you get started.

What is Task Tracker?

Task Tracker is a productivity platform that helps you create, organize, schedule, and track your tasks in one place.

Can I organize my tasks by deadlines?

Yes! You can assign due dates to tasks and view them easily through the Calendar to stay on top of your schedule.

Can I create tasks with my friends?

Absolutely! Use Group Tasks to collaborate with friends by adding them through their email or sharing an invitation link.

Can I see my completed tasks?

Yes. The History section keeps track of your previous tasks and activities, so you can look back at your progress.

Can I edit or delete a task after creating it?

Yes! You can easily update task details, change deadlines, mark tasks as completed, or delete them whenever needed.

Is my account and task information secure?

Yes. Your account information and tasks are protected through secure login and account settings.
